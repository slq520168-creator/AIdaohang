防骗指南

Scam guide

这页只讲怎么辨认常见骗局。不教怎么骗人。

How to spot common scams. This page does not teach fraud.

先记这三条

Three rules

  • 不转账、不看验证码、不装远程控制。
  • Do not transfer money, share codes, or install remote-control apps.
  • 赚钱任务要你先交定金，几乎都是骗。
  • A job that asks you to pay first is almost always a scam.
  • 催你发货币或礼品卡到「安全账户」，先挂断。
  • If someone wants crypto or gift cards sent to a “safe account”, stop.

刷单 / 好评

Fake tasks / fake reviews

先付小额再返现、用自己钱下单再报销、拉进企业群打款。正规平台不要你先打钱。

Pay-a-deposit-then-refund, buy-then-reimburse, or pay inside a chat group. Real platforms do not ask you to pay first.

假客服 / 假警察

Fake support / fake police

说账号风险、要装软件调查、转账到安全卡。银行和警察不会这么要。挂断回拨官方。

They claim your account is at risk and want remote software or a transfer. Hang up and call the official number.

投资盘

Fake investing

保本付息、小钱先赢再动员加仓。能提现的小额是钩。

Guaranteed profit and a small first withdrawal are bait. Do not send crypto to a stranger wallet.

网恋 / 杀猪盘

Romance scam

很快谈感情和赚钱，转到外部聊天再引去投资。散户后再要钱是续集。

Fast romance plus investing tips, then a move off-platform. More money after a “loss” is still the same scam.

敲诈

Extortion

拿照片要求转账。停止汇款，保存证据报警，不要再付「消毁费」。

Stop paying. Save evidence and report. Do not pay a “deletion fee”.

被骗了

If you already paid

  • 保存转账和聊天记录，挂失冻结，报警。不要找「回收公司」。
  • Save records, freeze the card, report. Ignore “recovery agents”.
